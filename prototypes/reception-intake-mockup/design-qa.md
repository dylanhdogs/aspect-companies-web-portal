# Reception Intake mockup — Version 2

## What was checked

- Compared the working page with the selected Version 2 mockup at **1698 × 926 px** and **100% display scale**.
- Reviewed the full page and a closer side-by-side view of the reminders, status buttons, filters, and inquiry list.
- Checked the layout at a phone-sized **390 × 844 px** screen. The page stays in place while the wide inquiry list can scroll inside its own area.
- Tried the main controls: status buttons, company filter, search, reminder filter, company link, inquiry details, empty results, and clearing filters.
- Checked the browser console; no errors appeared.
- Built the preview for production successfully.

## Results

- The page follows the chosen mockup: attention reminders first, then status buttons, filters, and a cross-company inquiry list.
- The sample names, companies, dates, and status counts now match the chosen design.
- Search and filters can be combined. Choosing a company narrows the list to that company, and the company link does the same.
- Selecting a contact opens a details panel with the inquiry status and next steps.
- Choosing a reminder shows the matching follow-ups. Clearing filters restores all seven sample inquiries.
- Empty results have a clear message and a button to restore the full list.
- On small screens, the layout stacks controls and keeps sideways scrolling inside the inquiry list.
